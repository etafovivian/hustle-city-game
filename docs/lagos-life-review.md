# Lagos Life review: what to learn, what to do better

Reviewed 2026-10-11 on lagoslife.app, signed in as the project owner (about 92,000 players online).

**Ground rules for the review:**
- We noted ideas and mechanics; we don't copy their art, text or code.
- We use fictional place names, not their real venues and brands.

## How their game works

| Area | What they do | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | You start in a room ("Hall of residence, UNILAG" or "Face-me-I-face-you, Mushin"), with a cooler, bucket, plastic chair and mattress. | Very relatable. The room grid grows with the home tier. |
| **Needs and mood** | Six bars: hunger, energy, fun, social, hygiene, toilet. A mood label ("Very Happy") and prompts such as "Eat something: tap the cooler or stove". | Sims-style loop. It gives players a reason to go places. |
| **Places** | About 25 real Lagos venues (amala joint, the Shrine, a tech hub, UNILAG, Jibowu Park, a stadium, a beach club…), plus Port Harcourt and Abuja. | Each place card has a story line, a menu of actions, who's there now, and player shops. |
| **Going places** | Tap a place, choose transport, tap **Go**: **Trek free, Keke ₦50, Danfo ₦50, Okada ₦200, Cab ₦450.** A 3D ride plays (chase camera, traffic, roadside stalls, billboards, danfo stickers like "No food for lazy man"), with **Skip**. | The ride makes the city feel big and shows billboards to everyone travelling. |
| **Inside a place** | A 3D room with players and NPCs (Mama Nkechi, Tunde), radio ambience ("Fuji music on the radio"), tap spots (buka counter, kitchen, wash bowl, Whot and Ludo bench) and live chat with the players there. | Multiplayer presence is a big part of the fun. |
| **Housing** | Weekly rent, a buy price, and a move-in cost of 3 weeks' rent ("pay the landlord and the agent"). The rent ladder is in the next table. You can hold homes in several cities, and Invest and Rent tabs let you own property and rent it out. | Real Lagos detail: the agent fee and the upfront move-in. |
| **Cars** | A dealership on Victoria Island, "from a Bajaj Boxer to a Bugatti": test drive, haggle, drive it home. | Status item; your own car replaces paid rides. |
| **Neighbours** | A list of players and their homes ("Mansion · Banana Island · 96 items"). Visiting knocks on the door; the owner lets you in. | Most players already own mansions, so their economy is far too easy. |
| **Player jobs ("Hustle")** | Players hire players: decorate, cook, DJ, drive, bodyguard, photographer, tutor, and masons, roofers and tilers to **build your house** in stages (foundation, walls, roof, finishing). The fee is held until the job is done. | Great social loop. **Moderation failure:** we saw public posts soliciting minors, sexual "DM me" posts and "message me for 100m" scams. |
| **Phone** | About 40 apps: Jobs, Messages, Bank, Invest, Houses, Cars, Health, Family, Staff, Company, Bus Co., Forbes rich list, Lagos Gist (news), Gov, Police, Court, Shops, **My Ads**, and **sponsored app tiles** marked NEW (real startups). | The sponsored tiles are a second ad product besides billboards. |
| **Money** | Billboards (real ads from ₦250k for 7 days; airport boards ₦500k), sponsored app tiles, brand partnerships (a real food-delivery app), "Play for real" card tables, betting apps and a casino. | We take billboards and sponsor tiles. **We won't do** betting, casino or "play for real" games; see "What we don't copy". |
| **Daily hook** | Daily gem hunt ("41,075 found · next prize ₦3,000"), a live "92k online" counter, and a "Gov" title for a top player. | Cheap, effective retention. |

## Their map, buildings and streets (second pass)

### Areas have a character
- **Mainland is everyday life:** amala joint, snooker spot, hair salon, viewing centre (football), the Shrine (music), wellness centre, tech hub, university, motor park, stadium, radio station.
- **Lagoon:** boat cruise jetty.
- **Island is the high life:** big market, High Court, gym, nightclub, pizza place, canopy walk, office towers, car dealer, five-star hotel, library, rooftop lounge, mall, casino, park, beach.
- **Player shops** are listed by area ("Player shops in Victoria Island": restaurants, pharmacies, provision stores, "from ₦750").
- **Fares rise with distance:** a cab costs ₦450 to Surulere and ₦550 to Victoria Island.

### Every enterable building is marked
- Each venue has a **round white icon badge floating above the roof** (pizza, saxophone, graduation cap, scales, car, bank…). A **Names** toggle adds a pill with the name next to each icon.
- Decorative buildings have no icon, so you can see at a glance what's enterable.
- Venues sit on their own plots with lawns, car parks, pools, courts and trees.
- **Billboards are huge** compared with buildings and sit between blocks and along the lagoon. They're the most visible thing on the map.

### Tapping a building opens a place card
- **The card holds:** a story line ("Lagos' most famous hotel…"), a share link, the **activities**, "Player shops here", and the transport picker with Go.
- **Activities are graded by luxury.** At the hotel: pool, poolside small chops, buffet, live band, shisha and gist, brunch, spa, lobby photo shoot, deluxe room, Presidential Suite. At the buka: amala and ewedu, jollof, pepper soup, efo riro, ofada, zobo, gist with Mama, learn the ewedu secret, help at the buka (work), wash hands, Whot with the regulars.
- **Inside**, it's a room scene with NPCs and players, ambient lines ("Fuji music on the radio", "Somebody asked for 'plenty pepper'"), tap spots and local chat.

### "Walk Yaba": a walkable street
- A ride takes you to Herbert Macaulay Way. You walk in third person: "Tap where you want to go. Hold and drag to steer."
- A **nearby bar** lists places with live distances: "Bus stop · 20 m, CcHub · 390 m, MindSpace · 460 m, Mallam Sani · 670 m, @Clarita48's house · 690 m". Tapping one walks you there. The location label updates as you go (Tejuosho Junction, then YabaTech Gate).
- Other players walk the same pavement. Ads appear on building walls, and a bus stop offers a danfo onward ("Ojuelegba · Stadium").

### The road, in detail (to copy the feel)
- **Dual carriageway:** a central **median with black-and-yellow striped kerbs**, trees planted along it, and lamp posts on it.
- **Markings:** white dashed lane lines, a double yellow centre line on undivided roads, and yellow danfos and BRT-style buses in the traffic.
- **Shoulder:** a **strip of red laterite earth** between the road edge and the pavement, the way real Lagos roads look.
- **Pavement:** wide, **grey stone or tile paving**, with lamp posts at the kerb.
- **Street front:** shops open straight onto the pavement with roller shutters, big painted signboards ("Victory Phones & Laptops", "Mama Ngozi Kitchen"), striped awnings and umbrella kiosks.
- **Street furniture:** a **bus stop shelter** with a bench, **power poles with sagging wires** across the street, and roadside traders under umbrellas.

## What this means for us (interactive buildings)

We can't enter buildings yet. The plan, in our 2.5D style:

1. **Icons on enterable buildings.**
   - A round badge with a category icon floats above each enterable building, with the name on a toggle or at close zoom.
   - Decorative houses stay plain.
   - Districts get named venues that fit them: an **event hall**, tailor and buka in Owambe Gardens; a **tech hub**, phone market and university gate in Tech Mile; a **motor park**, suya spot and market in Danfo Terminal; a **hotel**, rooftop lounge, mall and car dealer in Island Heights; a **jetty** and big market in Marina Wharf; a **beach club** in Lekki.
2. **Place cards for every venue:** story, graded activities (cheap to luxury), who's here, player shops in the area, transport and fare.
3. **Enter buildings: interior scenes.**
   - Reuse our isometric room renderer (the shop and decorate floor) for venue interiors: furniture art, NPC sprites, tap spots for activities, and ambient lines.
   - Every venue gets one room. Big venues (hotel, market) get 2–3 rooms.
4. **Walk mode on our map.**
   - At close zoom your avatar appears on the pavement. Tap a spot and it walks along the pavements, using our walk sprites and the road network.
   - It reaches a building's door and enters.
   - A "nearby" bar shows distances to venues, shops and friends' homes.
5. **Road upgrade, matching the list above:** median with striped kerbs and trees on the main roads, laterite shoulder, tiled pavements, bus stop shelters on danfo routes, power poles and wires, roadside umbrella traders, and shopfronts with signboards along the busiest street (Tech Mile is our "Herbert Macaulay").

## What we build, in order

### 1. "Go there" trips (next)
- **Place card:** tap a place, a district or a neighbour's shop to see its story, things to do, who's there, and transport choices with fares and travel time:

  | Mode | Fare | Speed |
  |---|---|---|
  | Trek | Free | Slow |
  | Keke | 50 coins | – |
  | Danfo | 50 coins | – |
  | Okada | 150 coins | Fast |
  | Cab | 400 coins | Fast, comfy |
  | Your own car | Fuel only | – |

- **The ride:** a short animated trip on our own map along the real roads, the camera following your vehicle, passing billboards (these count as billboard views), danfo stickers, and night lights after dark. **Skip** is always there.
- **Bridge:** the Island districts are reached over Third Mainland Bridge, which is our dream milestone.
- **Fares are City Coins:** this gives us a coin sink.

### 2. Homes, from poor to high life to crazy
Every player gets a home, separate from their shop. They move up by paying rent plus a move-in fee (landlord and agent, like Lagos). The home room reuses our decorate editor and grows with the tier:

| Tier | Example | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Face-me-I-face-you (Mushin Lane) | Shared corridor, bucket, mattress, small 6×6 |
| Student | Hall of residence (Akoka) | Bunk, books, cheap rent |
| Getting there | Self-contain (Yaba) | Own kitchen corner |
| Comfortable | Mini-flat (Lekki side) | Sitting room |
| High life | Terrace or family duplex (GRA) | Two floors, generator house, gate man |
| Big man / big madam | Ikoyi duplex | Pool, staff |
| **Crazy house** | Waterfront mansion, penthouse in Island Heights, "Banana-style" island villa | Showpieces: infinity pool, helipad, car gallery, gold everything. Only reachable through long play. |

- **Economy:** rent is the main coin sink. Prices must stay high relative to income so that mansions are rare and mean something; theirs are everywhere.
- **Later:** build your own house on a plot in stages (foundation, walls, roof, finishing). Our construction-kit spec already covers this.

### 3. Needs, the light version
Hunger, energy and fun only, fewer than theirs. Eating at a buka, resting at home and hanging out refill them. They nudge players to travel and visit places, never punish, and there's no toilet bar.

### 4. Cars
- A dealership district (Airport Ridge or Island Heights): Tokunbo Corolla, Camry, Lexus jeep, G-Wagon-style SUV, plus a Bajaj okada and a keke for starters.
- Owning a car makes trips free (fuel only), shows on your map pin, and parks outside your home.
- Art needed: Lagos yellow cab, private cars from 3 angles, a BRT bus.

### 5. In-game hustle board (City Coins only)
- Players hire players to decorate, cook for a party, DJ, drive, or build a house. The coins are held until the job is done.
- It needs strict moderation from day one: job types from a fixed list (no free-text titles), keyword and pattern filters, no open DMs, report button, rate limits, and 18+ only.
- Real money jobs stay in Stage B2 under PRD rules.

### 6. Presence and chat (row 9)
- See other players inside a place, with moderated chat limited to the people in that place, and knock-to-visit homes.

### 7. More revenue (safe)
- **Sponsored phone app tiles:** booked like billboards, with admin review.
- **Branded places:** for example a real buka chain's in-game outlet, clearly labelled.
- **Airport and bridge premium boards**, and billboards along ride routes.

## What we don't copy
- **No betting, casino or "play for real" games, and no betting-tips apps.** Nigerian regulation (lottery and gaming commissions) and the PRD's "no play to earn" stance rule them out, and they'd hurt trust.
- **No free-text public job posts or open DMs** without moderation. What we saw on their board is a child-safety and legal risk.
- **No real venue names or brands** without a partnership.

## Art we'll need (Stitch or Blender)
- **Vehicles:** yellow cab (front and back), Corolla, Camry, Lexus jeep, SUV, BRT bus, plus the missing danfo, okada and keke angles.
- **Home exteriors per tier:** face-me-I-face-you block, hall of residence, self-contain, mini-flat, terrace duplex, GRA family duplex, Ikoyi duplex, waterfront mansion, penthouse tower.
- **Home interiors per tier (isometric rooms):** shared-corridor room, student room, mini-flat sitting room, duplex lounge, mansion lounge with pool view.
- **Place interiors:** a buka, the hangout bar, a market stall row.
- **People:** more walk sheets (women and men, all 4 directions). The Blender character work covers this.

needs bars, then presence + venue chat (the big one).
