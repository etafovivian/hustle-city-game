First pass was read-only (wallet unchanged at ₦1,004,800); a second, owner-approved pass spent a little in-game money (see Trips and walking). ## How a place card works

Tapping a map pin opens a bottom sheet with:
- an icon, the full name and the area
- a one-line blurb
- a "Share a link to …" link
- **activity chips**: the preview of what you can do there
- **Here now**: player avatars currently at the venue (e.g. "Tunde, Femi")
- a **"Player shops here ›"** row: player-run shops attached to every venue
- a **ride picker** (Trek Free / Keke / Danfo / Okada / Cab), with Danfo preselected
- a rush-hour note, "⏰ Rush hour: Cab fares are up", shown with a ⏰ badge on Cab
- a green **"Go · ₦X"** button. If a venue is closed or locked, this shows "Closed till 10 PM" or "Members only 🔒" instead.

Yaba venues (CcHub, UNILAG, MindSpace) also have a "🚶🏾 Walk around Yaba ›" link.

Fares depend on distance from the player's location (the Boat Cruise at Five Cowries Creek). Keke and Danfo are always the same price because of the governor's active policy "Transport subsidy: Danfo and keke fares are half price". Fare ranges seen: Keke/Danfo ₦100–200, Okada ₦250–600, Cab ₦700–1,800.

## Map layers

- **Serious go-slow**: a live traffic indicator ("Traffic right now: Serious go-slow"), not a toggle. Combined with the rush-hour Cab surcharge.
- **Billboards**: a list of about 20 digital road billboards plus airport boards. Each billboard plays player-bought ads on rotation, 15 s each. Price from ₦250,000 per 7 days; MEGA boards; airport boards ₦500k per 7 days. Ad counts per board are shown (e.g. "43 ads on air"). The map is covered in real player ad images.
- **Neighbours**: a list of players with home type, area and item count (e.g. "Mansion · Banana Island · 113 items") and a **Visit** button ("drop by when they're home").
- **Sea Plots**: rent a grid of lagoon water tiles for ad images. ₦500 per plot per 7 days, with a width × height picker. Priced as "₦2,000 or $1.40", so there is a real-money option.
- **Gov**: the Lagos State House panel with Governor / Senator / President tabs. It shows the current governor (player), their slogan and vote count, an **active policy** (transport subsidy halving keke/danfo fares), the term end (Sun 8 PM), a 4-person cabinet (Finance, Works, Health, Transport commissioners), and the next week's election leaderboard. Candidates have slogans, campaign managers and vote %, and many slogans are promises like "Free 50 Billion".
- **Walk Yaba**: a prompt "Step inside Lagos Life · Herbert Macaulay Way, Yaba: walk past the tech hub, Tejuosho, the YabaTech gate and your friends' houses. First time: 0.4 MB." The button was "Walk there · Danfo ₦150", so walking mode requires travelling to Yaba first. Not entered (costs money).
- **Names**: toggles text labels on all pins.
- **City switcher**: Lagos / Port Harcourt / Abuja (see below).
