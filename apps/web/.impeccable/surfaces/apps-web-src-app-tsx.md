Scope: the whole Naija Hustle City web app shell (welcome/sign-in, Junction 101 map, Market Square, Work Hub, The Hangout, quests). Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience/job: Lagos 18–35s on mid-range Android in daylight, plus merchants; run the shop, clear quests, find real businesses. Constraints: coins never read as cash; commerce usable without the game; light assets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every place in Junction 101 is its own bolt of wax-print cloth and the selvedge prints the truth. Refuses the fintech default of white cards on grey with one green accent.

OWN-WORLD: Indigo-dyed shell (#1f2a6b), cadmium yellow, madder red, leaf green on a pale indigo-white ground; three authored two-colour wax-print repeats (Market Square concentric "record" circles, Work Hub ladder-brick, Hangout fan-and-dot). One variable grotesque (Archivo) for display and data, tabular numerals right-aligned. Hairline rules, not card soup.

STORY: The player understands coins are game-only, sees what to do next (serve, restock, claim), and finds real merchants in two taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indigo top bar (name lockup, coin balance in yellow, XP, account). Below, the Junction 101 map with each place wearing its print; quests as a folded bolt alongside on desktop, beneath on mobile. Primary action is tapping a place.

FORM: Wax-Print Selvedge, candidate 3 of 7 (seed 8210626b). Signature move: the printed selvedge strip on every location states its one rule; timed states are bars of exact remaining time; quest bolts unroll when claimable.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
