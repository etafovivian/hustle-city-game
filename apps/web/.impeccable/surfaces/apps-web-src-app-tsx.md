Scope: the whole Naija Hustle City web app shell (welcome/sign-in, Junction 101 map, Market Square, Work Hub, The Hangout, quests). Visitor mode: Operate (welcome/sign-in carries some Persuade).

Audience/job: Lagos 18–35s on mid-range Android, plus merchants; run the shop, clear quests, find real businesses. Constraints: coins never read as cash; commerce usable without the game; light assets.

User-pinned direction (2026-10-11): the user designed the welcome/sign-in in Google Stitch ("Lagos Street Arcade") and approved its look, logo and art. The brief wins over the earlier roll: dark-first arcade, Outfit, gold #ffc72c on midnight navy, the Stitch logo and isometric Lagos building art. The wax-print repeats, selvedge truth strip and exact-time bars carry over.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Junction 101 is a Lagos street arcade at night — gold-lit stalls, danfo and Ankara on midnight navy — where every surface still prints its one plain rule. Refuses both the fintech white-card default and the generic neon-cyber game skin.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight navy ground (#0a0f24) with navy-light panels (#121835) and input wells (#182042), gold (#ffc72c) as the one action and coin colour with a soft gold glow on primary CTAs, Ankara red (#df382b) and Naija green (#00a859) as accents. Outfit (self-hosted) for display and UI, heavy uppercase CTAs, tabular figures. Isometric hand-drawn Lagos buildings (Stitch art) on the map; the wax-print circle repeat on the welcome cloth; the gold-on-navy selvedge ticker.

STORY: A newcomer sees the logo and the street, understands coins are game-only, creates a shop in one screen (or browses as guest); a player sees the map's real-looking places and taps one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop welcome — left: wax-print cloth fading to navy, a navy card with the logo, NAIJA HUSTLE CITY lockup, three features with drawn icons, gold Browse merchants; selvedge at its foot. Right: New here / Sign in tabs, heading, coin line, the form, gold full-width CTA, guest link. Mobile welcome — street-scene hero with logo and tabs, selvedge band, then the form card. Signed in — top bar with logo, gold coin balance; the isometric Junction 101 map with name plates.

FORM: User-pinned "Lagos Street Arcade" (Stitch), superseding Wax-Print Selvedge (seed 8210626b) for type, palette and imagery. Signature move: gold-on-navy selvedge truth strip; isometric place art with name plates; exact-time bars.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
