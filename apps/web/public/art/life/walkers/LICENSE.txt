Walk-cycle sprite sheets rendered for Naija Hustle City by scripts/blender/agent/sprites.py (Blender 5.2, headless, LOOK=toon:
cel bands plus Freestyle outlines)
from the CC0 Quaternius bodies and animations in public/models (see public/models/LICENSE.txt), with clothes from
scripts/blender/agent/garments.py and hair from scripts/blender/hair.py. Original work for this project.
Each file: 8 frames of 96x160 in a row (Walk_Loop), direction se/sw/ne/nw = walking towards screen down-right,
down-left, up-right, up-left. Feet on a ground line 16 px above the frame bottom; 76 px per metre.

Outfit looks (one per body and outfit a player can wear, named <body>-<outfit>, e.g. man-tee_blue, woman-peplum_coral):
painted in the outfit colours of src/components/Avatar3D.tsx OUTFITS; agbada, kaftan, buba and peplum garments from
garments.py recoloured (the woman's kaftan is built on her body by sprites.py). Hair: man low_cut, woman braids, gele
with the peplum. Their pose sheets are in ../poses.

Staff in uniform (sprites.py STAFF_VARIANTS; for people at work in rooms only, never street walkers): nurse (light-blue
scrubs, white cap, bob hair), doctor (white coat open over a blue shirt and tie, stethoscope), chef (whites, white
apron, toque), waiter (white shirt, black waistcoat, long white apron) and bartender (dark shirt with rolled sleeves,
leather apron). Uniforms are painted on the body; the coat and aprons are lofted round the body and skinned (garments.py
Fit); the cap, toque and stethoscope are small meshes on the head and chest bones. Their work poses are in ../poses.
