Naija Hustle City · early build

A Lagos you can play.

Danfos round the roundabout, suya at night, owambe on Saturday, land in Lekki. A city game with a real marketplace inside: the shops, artisans and schools you meet in the game are real ones you can use.

Play it now Take the tour How you can help

Walk around the city

Nine areas, each with its own feel, laid out on real Lagos patterns: main roads, side streets, closes and plots.

Go inside and do things

25 places to enter, from Mama Nkechi's buka to the courtroom. Each room has people in it eating, dancing, serving and talking. Every place has a free thing to do, paid activities, and a big "flex" for players with money to show.

What's already built

Play

Your own shop

Every player runs a market shop: stock it, price it, decorate it, and sell to customers who walk in.

Real

A real marketplace

"Find a business" points players to real artisans and shops, with quote requests that go to the business.

Learn

Hustle Academy

Tech courses with server-checked lessons and quizzes, and a free certificate anyone can verify online. Run with Lampnet Institute of Technology.

Ads

Billboards

Real businesses book boards around the map. Banners are reviewed before they go up, and views are counted for the advertiser.

City

Life on the streets

Traffic that goes round the roundabout, people who use the pavements and zebra crossings, and day and night on Lagos time.

Share

Made to share

Links open on an area or a plot, certificates share to WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X, and every plot has a permanent id.

How it pays

Free to play. Money comes from businesses, learners and players who want more.

  • Billboards. Real businesses rent boards on the map, from ₦25,000 a week.
  • Hustle Academy. Free virtual certificate; printed certificate ₦5,000; professional training (Microsoft, AWS, DevOps, DevSecOps) ₦500,000–₦800,000 through Lampnet.
  • Sponsored places and courses. A bank, a telco or a school can have its own building and course in the city.
  • Land. Plots in prime areas, with a waiting list already open in the game.
  • Marketplace leads. Artisans and shops get customers from players; featured listings come later.

What's next

Getting around

Walk free or take a keke, danfo, okada or cab, with fares, rush hour and the odd "area boys" stop on the way.

Needs and moods

Hunger, energy and fun that the places in town fill, with Naija moods like "belle full".

Jobs and housing

Work shifts at the places you visit, and move up from face-me-I-face-you to a Lekki duplex.

See each other

Who's at a place right now, chat at venues, and friends' shops on the map.

More cities

Abuja and Port Harcourt, reached by flight or night bus.

Owning land

Build on your plot, rent it out, and see your name on the map.
